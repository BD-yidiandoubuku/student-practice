1. Introduction
We ("Student Practice") provide a mobile application to help students practice problems and track progress. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, and how to control it.

2. Data we collect
- Account information: name, email (via Google Sign-in).
- App usage: which problems attempted, answers, correctness, timestamps used to compute progress and provide features.
- Device information: basic device model and OS version for debugging and analytics.
- No sensitive personal data (e.g., health, race) is intentionally collected.

3. How we use data
- Provide core functionality (problem delivery, progress tracking).
- Improve service quality and fix bugs.
- Optional analytics to understand usage patterns.

4. Data sharing
- We do not sell personal data.
- We may share data with service providers that assist with hosting, analytics, or authentication (e.g., Google Sign-In). Such providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Data retention
- Attempts and progress are retained until the user requests deletion or account removal. Retention period: default 2 years (adjustable).

6. Your rights
- Access, correction, deletion requests: contact support@example.com
- You can revoke Google Sign-in permissions via your Google account settings.

7. Security
- We use industry-standard measures to protect data in transit and at rest. No absolute security guarantee.

8. Children
- If the app targets children under local law, obtain necessary parental consent. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16 without consent.

9. Changes to this policy
